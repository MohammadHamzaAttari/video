⏰ **What if time itself slows down?** ⏰

Einstein's general relativity predicts that time runs slower in stronger gravitational fields. Near a black hole, the curvature of spacetime becomes extreme — and time dilation becomes dramatic. If an astronaut orbited a supermassive black hole for just a few hours, they could return to Earth to find that years or even decades have passed. 🌌

🔭 **The physics:** The closer you get to a massive object, the slower your clock runs relative to someone in a weaker gravitational field. This isn't science fiction — it's been confirmed with precise atomic clocks on airplanes and satellites (GPS must correct for this effect daily!).

⚡ **The "mind blowing" part:** If you fell feet-first toward a black hole, the gravitational force on your feet would be vastly stronger than on your head ("spaghettification"). Meanwhile, your personal time would slow relative to the outside universe. You could experience what feels like a short journey while centuries pass on Earth.
