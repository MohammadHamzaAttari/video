## The Discovery (fact-check log — no older than 2 days at publish)

- Study: *Geophysical Research Letters* — Thanh-Son Pham (ARC DECRA Fellow,
  Australian National University)
- Coverage: The Conversation / ScienceDaily — **Aug 30–31, 2026**;
  ScienceBlog follow-up — **Sep 1, 2026**
- 362 glacial seismic events detected in Antarctic station data, 2010–2023
- 245 near the marine edge of Thwaites Glacier — most likely glacial
  earthquakes from capsizing icebergs
- Activity coincided with episodic acceleration of Thwaites' floating ice
  tongue — hint that ocean conditions may be destabilizing the ice
- Thwaites' full collapse ≈ 3 m of global sea-level rise
