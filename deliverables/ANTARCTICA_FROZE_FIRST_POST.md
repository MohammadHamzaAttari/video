## The Discovery (fact-check log)

- Study: *Science* — Prof Tom Gernon, Dr Thea Hincks et al., University of
  Southampton; coverage ScienceDaily **Aug 25 / retrieved Aug 31, 2026**
- Antarctica glaciated ~34 Ma; Arctic ~25 My later (polar asymmetry puzzle)
- Earth was ~5°C warmer then — ice "should have been impossible"
- Mechanism: **mantle waves** (recently discovered by Gernon's team; also
  linked to diamond volcano eruptions) — travel under continents after
  plate breakup, remove dense root rock, land rises
- Built the 2-km Dronning Maud Land escarpment + high plateau topped by the
  Gamburtsev Mountains (up to ~3,390 m, buried under 1–3 km of ice)
- By 34 Ma nearly half the range stood above 2 km — snow survived summer
- Ice-albedo feedback cooled the globe ~1°C
- East Antarctic Ice Sheet holds ~52 m of sea-level equivalent
- 100-million-year landscape evolution simulations
