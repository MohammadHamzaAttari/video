## Animated 3D source clips used in the v3 edit

The three GIFs below were newly acquired for this edit from Wikimedia Commons' public-domain mirror of NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) release 14132. They are genuine moving visual assets (not frame dumps); their animation frames are retained and re-edited in the Reel. No source-audio is present or used.
